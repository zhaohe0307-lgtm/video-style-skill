# Video style quality checklist

Use this checklist against the selected profile, not against personal taste.

## Profile integrity

- Confirm the current prompt, profile, preset, and defaults were applied in the correct priority.
- Confirm no critical voice, brand, font, aspect-ratio, asset, or renderer choice was silently replaced.
- Record any approved deviation.

## Story and narration

- Verify the opening promise matches the video's actual content.
- Check that spoken ideas follow a natural causal or conversational order.
- Listen for repetitive pause lengths, abrupt fragments, mispronunciations, and stale audio files.
- Confirm the rendered voice is the selected voice.

## Cover and frames

- View the cover at phone size and identify the first visual center immediately.
- Check title contrast, safe areas, hierarchy, and supporting-text restraint.
- Inspect one settled frame per scene for clipping, overlap, unintended density, and style drift.

## Visuals, captions, and motion

- Verify palette, type, shapes, borders, shadows, icons, and imagery use the profile tokens.
- Check caption line count, timing, contrast, safe area, and highlight behavior.
- Confirm visual changes follow spoken meaning throughout each scene.
- Remove unexplained motion, repeated entrance tricks, long frozen holds, and transitions that fight the narration.

## Audio and output

- Check voice clarity, music ducking, effect levels, peaks, and intentional silence.
- Confirm canvas, frame rate, duration, codec, filename, and versioning.
- Preserve previously delivered renders during revisions.

## HyperFrames addendum

- Run `hyperframes check` after HTML changes and resolve every error.
- Confirm root, audio, clips, effects, and timelines share the final duration.
- Snapshot the cover and one settled frame per scene.
- Seek across the full composition to catch late empty frames and reveal drift.
- Render only after the profile and snapshots agree.
